# Menu transcription

Source: current `MENU.pdf`, 16 image-only spreads, visually re-audited in enlarged panels on 2026-09-09. The downloadable copy is byte-identical. There are 124 dish groups / 174 priced variants in 17 categories. Prices use integer euro cents. No prices or dishes came from the old website or Mai Wok. Items 286–291, the Inside Out preparation note, and Frischkäse for item 314 were added from the current supplied menu and the user's corrections.

Source details retained deliberately:

- The flyer numbers Udon as 28 and Dorayaki as 29, and omits several other numbers. Numbers have not been renumbered.
- The current menu calls the dessert page “Nachtisch”. The website uses “Desserts” to keep the German navigation concise and consistent.
- Tofu ramen says “Vegan auf Anfrage” while the shared description includes egg. It is not marked vegan or silently prepared vegan. Customers can request changes in the order notes.
- Taki is under the vegetarian heading but has b, g, d allergen codes. The website preserves the source classification and codes, and advises customers to discuss allergies with HANA. No allergy-free claims are made.
- Source set counts are inconsistent: Familie says 78 pieces while its listed components total 72. Other sets include extra nigiri beyond the headline count. Headline counts and component lists are retained, rather than silently changing the offer.
- Allergen codes are the flyer annotations, not an independently complete ingredient analysis. Some descriptions name ingredients absent from their source codes. The PDF remains available to customers.
- The flyer lists different opening hours. The previously supplied project schedule remains authoritative for this menu/booking task, as recorded in README.md.

The reservation and pickup forms prepare email requests. A configured restaurant booking/delivery service is required for direct online transmission and live capacity confirmation.
