# HANA Restaurant

## Register

brand

## Users
German-speaking diners in Olsberg, using phones to find food, opening hours and reservations, and desktops to plan visits.

## Product Purpose
A fast static restaurant website with the supplied flyer menu, variant selection, pickup-order preparation and table-reservation requests.

## Brand Personality
Considered, welcoming, precise. Photography from the existing HANA website.

## Anti-references
No copied Mai Wok design, heavy sliders, invented menu prices or unverified contacts.

## Design Principles
The detailed user brief governs the implementation: German customer copy, prominent actions, accurate data, fast delivery, accessible interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, native dialogs, visible focus, 44px targets, reduced motion, readable contrast.
